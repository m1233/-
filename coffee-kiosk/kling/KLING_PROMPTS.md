# Kling — ~3-minute kiosk film (construction → completed → interior → first order)

18 clips × 10 s + 5 s end card ≈ **3:05**. Settings for every clip: **9:16**, **10 s**, Professional / highest quality, Creativity low (Relevance high).
Images: `kiosk_construction.jpg` (start) and `kiosk_completed_clean.jpg` (finished kiosk, TikTok overlays removed).

Append to every prompt:
`Photorealistic real footage, shot on iPhone, natural daylight, realistic textures, keep the exact same building design, signage, logo and colors.`

Negative prompt (every clip):
`cartoon, CGI, 3D render, illustration, blurry, distorted hands, extra fingers, warped or changed text, new logos, watermark`

  `The same kiosk under construction, steel frame now closed with cement board walls, grey stucco half applied, no signs yet, scaffolding, workers, same metal canopy and same camera angle, photorealistic`
- **B — interior** (reference: `kiosk_completed_clean.jpg`):
  `Interior of this exact drive-thru coffee kiosk, 3 by 5 meters, view from the staff door down the narrow aisle, white walls, black quartz countertops, warm oak cabinets, charcoal subway-tile backsplash, grey porcelain floor, LED ceiling panels, a backlit black sign with the same logo, professional espresso machine and grinders, the drive-thru service window on the right, photorealistic, natural light`
